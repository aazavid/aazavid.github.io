An AI service for finding interesting places for road trips and weekend getaways. Planning a car trip usually means jumping between maps, reviews, and scattered lists—without a clear sense of which stops are actually worth the detour for a given route and timeframe.

Pause

iOS Prototype / technical validation

My iOS project for more intentional phone use. Opening an app is often automatic—a habit rather than a choice. The idea is a short pause before the familiar open, so you can notice the automatic action and consciously decide whether to continue.

My contribution covers the product hypothesis, PRD, user scenarios, and prototype development. Status: Prototype / technical validation. This is early technical and product exploration; the app is not released and is not in the App Store.
